HEALTH CONDITIONS AND SPECIAL CARE

DOGS ON MEDICATION
Medications must be given at consistent times each day; set a daily reminder.
Morning medication administration typically takes 5-10 minutes (including treats/pill pockets).
Some medications require administration with food; coordinate with feeding schedule.
Track medication doses in a log; note any side effects to report to the vet.

DOGS WITH JOINT ISSUES / ARTHRITIS
Replace high-impact exercise with gentle, low-impact activities: short slow walks (15-20 min), swimming.
Apply warm compress to stiff joints before walks (10 minutes).
Ramps or steps to help dogs onto furniture or into cars.
Avoid stairs when possible.
Joint supplements (fish oil, glucosamine, chondroitin) given daily with meals.

OVERWEIGHT PETS
Measure food portions precisely at every meal; free-feeding should be avoided.
Increase exercise gradually: add 5 minutes per week to existing walk duration.
Weigh pet weekly and log results (5 minutes).
Use low-calorie treats or replace treats with vegetables (carrots for dogs).

ANXIOUS DOGS
Maintain strict, predictable daily routines to reduce anxiety.
Calming walks in quiet areas (20-30 min) are more beneficial than chaotic environments.
Enrichment activities like sniff walks or puzzle feeders (15-20 min) reduce stress.
Consider a calming supplement with meals if recommended by a vet.
Avoid leaving high-anxiety dogs alone for more than 4 hours.

PETS WITH DIETARY RESTRICTIONS
Stick strictly to prescribed diet; note any foods to avoid.
Hydration monitoring may be required; track water intake daily (5 minutes).
Prescription food must be measured precisely at each meal.
