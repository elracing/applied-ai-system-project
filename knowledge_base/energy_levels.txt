PET CARE BY ENERGY LEVEL

HIGH ENERGY DOGS (border collies, huskies, labs, vizslas, jack russells)
High energy dogs need 60-90 minutes of vigorous exercise per day, split across 2-3 sessions.
Mental stimulation is critical: add puzzle feeders, sniff walks, or training sessions (15-20 minutes each).
Without adequate exercise, high energy dogs develop destructive behaviors.
Recommended tasks: long morning walk (30-40 min), training session (15-20 min), evening walk/run (30 min), enrichment activity (15 min).

MEDIUM ENERGY DOGS (golden retrievers, cocker spaniels, beagles, standard poodles)
Medium energy dogs need 30-60 minutes of exercise per day.
Two walks of 20-30 minutes each is typically sufficient.
Weekly enrichment activities like fetch or swimming are beneficial.
Recommended tasks: morning walk (20-30 min), evening walk (20-30 min), weekly grooming session (20 min).

LOW ENERGY DOGS (bulldogs, basset hounds, shih tzus, chow chows)
Low energy dogs need 20-30 minutes of gentle exercise per day.
Short, slow walks are preferred over intense activity.
These breeds are prone to overheating; exercise in cool parts of the day.
Recommended tasks: gentle morning walk (15 min), short afternoon walk (10-15 min), grooming (15 min).

CATS (indoor)
Indoor cats need 2 play sessions of 10-15 minutes per day to stay mentally and physically healthy.
Interactive toys like feather wands prevent boredom.
Litter box must be scooped daily (5 minutes) and fully cleaned weekly (15 minutes).
Recommended tasks: morning play session (15 min), evening play session (15 min), litter scoop (5 min).
