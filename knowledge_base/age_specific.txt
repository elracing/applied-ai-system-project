AGE-SPECIFIC PET CARE

PUPPIES (under 1 year)
Puppies need 5 minutes of exercise per month of age, twice per day (e.g., 3-month-old = 15 min twice daily).
Puppies should not be over-exercised as their growth plates are still developing.
Socialization is critical: 15-20 minutes of exposure to new environments, people, and animals daily.
Puppies need 3-4 small meals per day.
Potty breaks every 2 hours during the day (5 minutes each).
Basic training sessions of 5-10 minutes, 2-3 times per day are highly recommended.

ADULT DOGS (1-7 years)
Adult dogs thrive on consistent routines. Morning and evening walks are the foundation.
Annual vet check-up (schedule 30 minutes for travel and visit preparation).
Monthly flea/tick prevention medication (5 minutes to administer).
Heartworm prevention medication monthly (5 minutes).

SENIOR DOGS (7+ years)
Senior dogs need shorter, more frequent walks rather than one long session.
Joint supplements (like fish oil or glucosamine) administered daily with meals (5 minutes).
More frequent vet visits: every 6 months. Schedule vet check-ups.
Mental enrichment becomes more important as physical activity decreases: puzzle feeders (15 min), gentle sniff walks (20 min).
Watch for signs of pain or stiffness; reduce walk intensity if needed.
Senior dogs may need help with grooming as they become less flexible.
Dental care becomes more critical with age; daily tooth brushing (5 minutes) is essential.

KITTENS (under 1 year)
Kittens need 3 meals per day of kitten-formulated food.
Multiple short play sessions (10 minutes each, 3-4 times per day) to develop coordination.
Socialization with people during the first 3 months is critical for temperament.

SENIOR CATS (10+ years)
Senior cats may develop arthritis; ensure litter box has low entry points.
More frequent grooming help may be needed (10-15 min daily).
Increase vet visits to twice yearly.
