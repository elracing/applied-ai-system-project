GENERAL PET CARE GUIDELINES

DAILY FEEDING
Dogs should be fed 2 times per day (morning and evening). Each meal typically takes 10-15 minutes.
Puppies under 6 months need 3-4 meals per day.
Cats can be fed 2 times per day or allowed free-feeding dry food.
Fresh water must always be available and bowls cleaned daily (5 minutes).

WALKS AND EXERCISE
Adult dogs need at least 2 walks per day, totaling 30-60 minutes depending on breed and energy level.
A morning walk of 20-30 minutes and an evening walk of 20-30 minutes is a common routine.
High-energy dogs (huskies, border collies, labs) need 60-90 minutes of exercise per day.
Low-energy dogs (bulldogs, basset hounds) need only 20-30 minutes of gentle walking per day.

GROOMING
Brushing reduces shedding and matting. Short-haired dogs need brushing 1-2 times per week (10 minutes each).
Long-haired dogs need daily brushing (15-20 minutes).
Nail trimming is needed every 3-4 weeks and takes about 15 minutes.
Ear cleaning should be done weekly for floppy-eared breeds (10 minutes).
Baths are needed every 4-6 weeks and take 20-30 minutes.

DENTAL CARE
Brushing a dog's teeth daily reduces dental disease significantly. Each session takes 5 minutes.
Dental chews can supplement brushing and take about 10 minutes for the dog to consume.
